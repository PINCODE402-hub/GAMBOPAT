About Gambopat

Where all metals surrender. Crafted to last.

Who we are

Gambopat Engineering & Training Center specialises in precision light welding that combines quality, efficiency and innovation. From custom fabrication and repairs to industrial projects, our skilled engineers deliver work you can rely on. We also train the next generation through hands-on apprenticeships.
