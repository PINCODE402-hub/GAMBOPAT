ABOUT US

At Gambopat Engineering & Training Center, we specialize in precision light welding solutions that combine quality, efficiency, and innovation. Whether it's custom fabrication, repairs, or industrial projects, our skilled engineers and technicians deliver work you can rely on. But we don't stop at just doing the work, we empower the next generation of engineers and welders through hands-on training programs designed to turn skills into mastery. From beginners to professionals, our center provides the knowledge, tools, and guidance needed to excel in the welding industry.
